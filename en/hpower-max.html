Special features

All the features of HPower Max

Exclusive features and developments that combine full kosher standards with everything you need from an advanced player.

Airtight security

An exclusive, airtight security mechanism with three layers of protection – no way to break in or swap the version.

Smart gallery

An advanced gallery with a smart scanning mechanism that shows and opens only your own recordings and screen recordings – any other video is blocked. Includes smart content-based classification, similar-photo detection, a text-from-image engine and advanced editing.

Custom version

Choose and manually adjust the version's kosher level, including a version without games, without installs or without kosher radio.

Face recognition

A smart built-in face recognition system that unlocks the device with a glance – quickly and securely.

Quick search

A built-in quick search mechanism: search your whole device in seconds – files and apps, including inside document contents.

Advanced kosher store

An advanced kosher store with 300 leading apps and games, including all the useful apps – Google Keyboard, Google Translate, screen translation, maps and navigation, public transport and more. Includes app data backup and restore.

Smart active video blocking

A smart system that blocks video and video apps in real time, with support for recordings and external installs.

Every AirPods feature

A built-in, exclusive mechanism that unlocks every AirPods feature on the device, including noise cancellation, head gestures, adaptive audio, ear detection, an equalizer and more.

Bluetooth fixer

A built-in Bluetooth address fixer that solves connection problems between devices with the same Bluetooth address.

New

Smart voice assistant

A smart AI-powered voice assistant that performs a range of quick actions on the device by voice, including voice typing, playing songs, installing apps, searching and sharing files, navigation, taking photos and videos, and more.

Themes

Personalize the system's look: colors, icons, tiles, fonts and the lock screen.

Kosher radio

Kosher radio that broadcasts only Haredi stations.

Fast file sharing

An exclusive mechanism for sharing files and folders at up to 40MB per second, between HPower devices.
