FAQ

Got a question? We've got answers

Where is The Lab and what are your opening hours?

The Lab is at 31 Nahal Sorek St., Beit Shemesh. Opening hours: Sunday to Thursday, 17:00–21:00.

What computer repair services do you offer?

We handle hardware and software faults, upgrades, virus removal, optimization and performance tuning, as well as operating system and software installation.

Can you recover data from a hard drive, memory card or Android phone?

Yes. We recover and extract data from hard drives, removable drives, memory cards, computers and Android devices. The chance of success depends on the device's condition, so we recommend you stop using it immediately and bring it to The Lab as soon as possible.

What's the difference between the HPower kosher Android players?

Every model includes exclusive video blocking in every app with app installs left open, an advanced kosher app store and exclusive kosher radio. HPower Max is the most advanced kosher player, with 32GB of storage, a store of 300 apps and games, and an exclusive three-layer security mechanism. See the full model comparison.

Can I get a custom version or blocking setup?

Yes. We develop custom Android versions, blocking tools and apps for private customers and businesses.

Can I buy the paid apps separately?

Paid apps and services aren't sold separately – only as part of selected versions and models. Free apps such as Kosher FM, VidBlocker, Smart Gallery and System Guard are available to download on the site.
